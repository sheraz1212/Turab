# Reference: "The Scientist's Insane Time Test" (40 s, 720x1280, 30 fps)
- Only ~4 hard cuts in 40 s (7.5, 23.7, 32.1, 35.9). The rest is ONE moving camera that flies from scene to scene.
- Frame-difference motion averages 10-30 (very high) every second: the camera never stops.
- Moves: spinning/rolling fall WITH the character (camera tracks the falling body, tower whipping past),
  push into a pupil/eye -> inside the head (brain at a desk), push through a round porthole window -> next set,
  orbit around a group (support-group circle), fast dolly past characters in a line, push-in on a prop (wrist screen) until it fills the frame,
  blueprint board pan/zoom, crane up the tower, pull back from a lit display, push into a hat/door to transition.
- Transitions are match-moves: the camera dives INTO an object (eye, window, door) and comes out in the next scene.
- Speed: a new framing every ~1-1.5 s, achieved by motion not cuts. Close character framing, heads big.
