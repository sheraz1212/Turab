# Reference: "Honey on His Shoulders Every Morning" (68 s, 720x1280, 30 fps)

Story: a prisoner smears honey on his shoulders every morning, which attracts flies. A guard takes the jar away and the flies follow it into the royal feast. The king swats them with his sword, comes down to the cell, and taps a fly on each shoulder. That counts as a knighting, so the prisoner goes free. Our version is an ORIGINAL script that keeps the same plot beats.

## Look
- 2D bean-head cards (thick dark ink line + **white sticker outline**, freckles, blush), standing in a detailed 3D set.
- Dungeon: green stone blocks with ink edges, warm orange brick behind iron bars, straw floor, a skylight grate with god rays, floating dust motes.
- Throne room: red/gold banners, green walls, a long table (roast chicken, soup, grapes, apples), tall red thrones.
- Palette: olive-green + warm orange, glossy. The honey is shiny golden yellow with drips.
- Fly swarms: lots of small black flies (with wings) as billboards, plus a hero fly on the nose and on the shoulders.
- Captions: lowercase rounded bold white with a dark shadow, 2–3 words at a time, at ~25% (shot top) or ~60–75% height.
- Pop text: "NO MORE HONEY!" and "KNIGHTED!" — red text in a white box, 3D style, top of frame.

## Cast
- Prisoner: teal ragged tunic with a red patch, rope belt, cuffs, spiky hair, smug half-closed eyes.
- Guards: green uniform + green peaked cap.
- Warden/captain: red coat with gold buttons, black bicorne, white mutton-chop sideburns, fat body.
- King: big jewelled crown, white curly beard, red robe with ermine.
- Queen: tall white curly wig, pearl necklace.

## Camera and cuts (avg shot ~1.8 s, the camera always moving)
1. 0–3 s: medium shot, the prisoner pours honey (slow push-in).
2. 3–6 s: wider, with the ceiling grate; guards behind the bars.
3. Close-up of the face and honey on the shoulders → he kneels → flies swarm in (push-in on the face).
4. Over-the-shoulder shots from behind the prisoner toward the guards.
5. Macro of the fly on the nose (he doesn't blink; static, comic).
6. Wide shot with a Dutch tilt: the captain shouts, plus the pop text.
7. Two-shot, the captain grabs the jar → a shocked face → he runs, with flies trailing.
8. Low wide shot looking up at the skylight → a cut into the throne room.
9. Close-ups of the king and queen → a wide table shot → the king swings his sword (tilted angle).
10. In the cell, the dinner falls through the grate. Looking UP through the floor grate at the king's angry face.
11. Low angle up the stairs, the king storms down; a tracking shot down the corridor with guards.
12. The king behind the prisoner, the sword raised (OTS), then close-ups of each shoulder with a fly as the sword hits.
13. Extreme close-up of the shoulder (the sword fills the frame) → "Thank you" with a smug face → the king's shocked face.
14. KNIGHTED! pop → the final wide shot, everyone smiling, the chains gone.

## Motion
Mouths talk, eyebrows react, the body bobs. A shake and a flash on every hit, plus a pop on each text reveal. Light flicker and dust motes in every shot.
