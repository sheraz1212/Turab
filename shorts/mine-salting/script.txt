For three years, Jake dug for gold, and found nothing.
So he did what desperate miners did. He salted the mine.
He loaded a shotgun with gold dust, and blasted it into the rock.
Now the walls sparkle.
He nails up a sign. For sale. Rich vein.
The next morning, a millionaire rolls up in a shiny new car. And he brought an expert.
The expert smashes a chunk of rock, swirls it in a pan, and squints. Gold flakes. Everywhere.
You see, salting worked because the gold was real. It just didn't come from that mine.
The millionaire pays ten thousand dollars, cash.
Jake and his partner run off laughing. They can't believe somebody fell for it.
But the millionaire doesn't leave.
He lights a stick of dynamite, tosses it at the back wall like a firecracker, and runs.
Boom.
When the dust clears, there it is. A vein of gold as thick as your arm.
Jake had stopped digging ten feet too early.
