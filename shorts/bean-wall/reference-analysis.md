# Reference: "The Prisoner Who Fed the Wall" (66 s, 720x1280, 30 fps)
Style only; our script is original (beans, own lines).
- Cuts ~every 1.3-1.5 s (~45 shots). Camera very close, eye level or slightly low; push-ins on faces.
- Sets: teal/blue-green stone cell with a jagged black crack (filled with yellow peas); brick guard room with
  bars and lanterns; warden's office; ticket stall with a big painted sign; dark blue night cell; macro of
  swelling peas; final burst + sunset field outside with peas everywhere.
- Cast: skinny prisoner (black/white stripes, striped cap, sly half-lid eyes); green-cap guards; fat warden
  (red coat, bicorne, white mutton chops, keys); visitors at the stall.
- Beats: prisoner spoons peas into crack + cup of water -> guards laugh -> guard waves a sausage, prisoner
  drools -> last pea -> warden laughs, pop box DOUBLE HELPINGS -> prisoner eats double, licks spoon ->
  "growing a beanstalk" guards gasp -> 3 weeks, pop box 10,000 PEAS -> ticket stall -> guard steals a
  fistful, hides them in a jug -> "the wall is thirsty" (finger up) -> warden roars, soaks the crack ->
  night, guards sleep (Zzz) -> wall creaks, macro peas swell -> burst, peas flood -> walks out at sunset.
- Text: white one-word captions low-centre; red/white pop-text boxes at top.
