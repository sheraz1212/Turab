Every night, this skinny prisoner pushes his dinner into the wall.
One dry bean at a time, he packs them into a tiny crack.
Then he pours in his water and whispers, "Grow big."
The guards think he has lost his mind.
"Feeding the wall?" one laughs, waving a sausage under his nose.
The prisoner just smiles and gives the wall his last bean.
When the warden hears about it, he laughs so hard his buttons pop.
"Give him double beans!" he shouts. "The wall looks hungry!"
So the prisoner gets double. And the crack gets fuller.
Weeks pass. Ten thousand beans later, the guards start selling tickets.
"Come see the Bean Wall!"
But one hungry guard keeps stealing beans for his soup.
So the prisoner shouts, "The wall is thirsty!"
The warden grabs a bucket and soaks the crack himself.
That night, everyone sleeps.
And the wall starts to creak.
You see, dry beans soaked in water swell up with huge force.
Packed tight in a crack, ten thousand of them push like a giant fist.
With one loud crack, the wall splits open,
and the prisoner the warden fed walks right out.
