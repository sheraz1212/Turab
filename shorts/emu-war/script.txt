In nineteen thirty-two, the Australian army went to war... against birds.
Out in Western Australia, twenty thousand emus march onto the farms, and eat all the wheat.
The farmers beg for help. So the army sends a major, two soldiers, and two machine guns.
Everyone laughs. Machine guns? Against giant chickens?
But the emus have a plan. They split into tiny groups, and scatter.
The soldiers chase them in a truck. The emus run fifty kilometres an hour, straight through the bushes. The truck can't keep up.
So they set an ambush at a dam. A thousand emus walk right into it. The gunner opens fire, and the gun jams.
After a month, the army has fired ten thousand bullets. About ten bullets for every bird they hit.
You see, an emu is fast, tough, and it never stays in one big crowd.
And here's the crazy part.
The major said, if he had soldiers who could take bullets like those birds, he could beat any army in the world.
The army went home. The emus won.
