This welder spent all night building walls inside an empty fuel tank.
Five of them, each with a big round hole in the middle.
His boss nearly fainted. Every wall stole space, and every gallon of space was money he would never haul.
Down the road, a rival driver did the opposite: a tank with nothing inside but fuel.
More gallons, more cash. By winter, he was buying lunch for the whole truck stop.
Then came the mountain pass, covered in ice.
Both drivers spotted a rock slide ahead and slammed the brakes.
The welder's truck groaned, and stopped.
The smooth truck kept going... sliding... until its bumper hung over the cliff.
You see, a full tanker carries about twenty-five tons of fuel. When the truck stops, the fuel doesn't.
In a smooth tank, it rushes forward in one giant wave and shoves the whole truck ahead.
The walls break that wave into tiny splashes, so the fuel has nothing to push with.
Next spring, the rival paid the welder to fill his tank with walls.
