Every morning, the whole town of Oakvale walks to the square, and stares at a lever.
Nobody knows what it does. There's just one rule. Never pull it.
The founders built everything in a perfect spiral around it. The streets, the houses, even the bakery.
For a hundred years, nobody touched it.
Then a girl named Nora finds a dusty map in her grandpa's attic. The spiral streets aren't really streets. They're channels.
The whole town sits on top of an old, dry river.
Her teacher laughs. The mayor says she's making it up.
But that spring, the rain doesn't stop. A wall of water comes rushing down the hills, straight at Oakvale.
Everyone panics. Nora runs to the square, and pulls the lever.
Deep underground, giant stone gates grind open.
The flood hits the spiral, swirls around every house, and pours into a hidden lake under the town.
Not a single home floods.
You see, the founders built Oakvale on the river on purpose. The streets were the drain.
And here's the crazy part.
The lever was never a secret. The town just forgot why it was there.
