**Title:** How a Beach Hole Becomes the Iwo Jima Tunnels
**Length:** ~68 sec | 1080x1920 | Voice: ElevenLabs (calm documentary)

> Facts note: Iwo Jima par Japanese soldiers ne 1944–45 mein ~18 km tunnels khodi thin. Andar volcanic garmi aur sulfur ki gas thi (log thori thori der ki shifts mein kaam karte the). Hamle se pehle ~70 din bombing hui. Marines 19 Feb 1945 ko utre; flamethrowers se caves par hamla kiya. Script inhi baaton par hai — upload se pehle ek dafa Wikipedia par numbers confirm kar lein.

## Voiceover script

> You dig a hole in the black sand to hide from the bombs.
> Planes roar right over your hideout.
> So you cover it with rocks and ash. From the sky, it's invisible.
> But a bomb hits nearby, and the sand walls collapse.
> So you mix volcanic ash into cement. It dries as hard as stone.
> Now you dig sideways.
> But the deeper you go, the hotter it gets. The island is a volcano.
> The rock burns at over 120 degrees.
> So you dig in five-minute shifts, and blow in air through hidden shafts.
> Then sulfur gas starts leaking from the walls.
> So you cut vents that push it out, low, like steam from the ground.
> When the Marines land, they spray fire into your entrance.
> So you build every tunnel with sharp turns. The flames stop at the first corner.
> You add levels. A hospital. A command room. Storage.
> Until it stretches eighteen kilometers under one tiny island.
> They seal an entrance with explosives.
> But there are hundreds of exits, and you walk out behind them.
> Congratulations. You have built the Iwo Jima tunnels.

(~175 words, ~66–70 sec)
