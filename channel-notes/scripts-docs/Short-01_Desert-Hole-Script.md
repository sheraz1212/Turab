# Short #01 — How a Desert Hole Becomes an Underground Home

**Title (YouTube):** How a Desert Hole Becomes an Underground Home
**Length:** ~65 sec | **Format:** 1080x1920, 24 fps | **Voice:** ElevenLabs (calm, deep, documentary male/female)

**Topic kyun chuna:** Desert scene Blender mein sab se halka hai (ret, pathar, aasmaan — jungle ke hazaron trees nahi). Aapke laptop (Intel UHD 620) par ye aaram se render ho jayega. Story ka pattern bilkul Cu Chi wali video jaisa hai: Problem → Hal → Nayi problem → Naya hal → Bara reveal → Twist → Payoff.

---

## 1. Full Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs mein ye paste karein)

> You're lost in the desert, and a sandstorm is coming.
> So you dig a hole in the sand to survive.
> But the walls keep collapsing.
> So you line them with flat stones, and finally it holds.
> Inside, it's cooler. But at noon, it's still burning hot.
> So you dig deeper. Every meter down, the air gets colder.
> Then the sand turns wet. Water.
> You dig a narrow well, and now you never go thirsty.
> But down here, it's pitch dark.
> So you cut a tiny hole in the roof and drop in a piece of glass. Sunlight pours in.
> The air gets stale. So you build a tall tower above the entrance. It catches the wind and pushes fresh air down.
> You carve a bedroom. A cold room for food. A second exit.
> Until one hole becomes an entire hidden home.
> Then one night, a flash flood hits the desert.
> Water rushes toward the entrance.
> But you built the doorway higher than the ground, and dug a drain into the sand.
> The water vanishes.
> Congratulations. You just survived the desert. Underground.

**Words:** ~165 | **Andaza time:** 60–68 sec (ElevenLabs speed 1.0–1.05)

## 3. Captions (CapCut)

- **Style:** ek waqt mein **sirf ek word**, safed, mota font (Montserrat ExtraBold ya "Bangers"), kaali outline/shadow.
- **Jagah:** surface aur mitti ki line ke bilkul upar (screen ka ~35% height).
- **Zaroori numbers alag highlight (peela, bara):** `45°C`, `22°C`, `WATER`, aur end par "UNDERGROUND".
- CapCut: Auto Captions → "1 word" style → position set.

## 4. Blender Setup (aapke laptop ke liye)

**Render settings**
- Engine: **Eevee** (Cycles nahi)
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (agar bohat slow ho to 720x1280 render karein)
- Frame rate: **24 fps** → 65 sec = ~1560 frames
- Samples: 16–32
- Output: PNG sequence (agar render beech mein ruk jaye to wahin se shuru ho sake), phir CapCut/FFmpeg se video

**Scene ke objects (sab simple shapes)**
- **Zameen ka block:** ek bara Cube, front face kati hui. Ret ki texture (Poly Haven: "aerial sand", "coast sand"). Tehein (layers) dikhane ke liye 3–4 rang ki patti.
- **Gaddha / kamre:** Boolean modifier (Cube se kaat kar). Animation ke liye cutter cube ka size keyframe karein → gaddha "grow" hota dikhega.
- **Pathar:** 5–6 low-poly rocks, array kar ke, Scale 0 → 1 keyframe (pop-in).
- **Character:** Mixamo se ek character + "Digging" aur "Walking" animation.
- **Sandstorm / dhuan:** pehle **image/video overlay** CapCut mein lagayein (Blender smoke simulation aapke PC par bohat slow hogi).
- **Roshni ki shaft:** Spot light + "Volume Scatter" sirf us scene ke liye.
- **Paani:** ek flat plane, neela, halki chamak — simulation ki zarurat nahi.
- **Hawa ke arrows:** simple safed arrow shapes, "Follow Path" constraint par chalayein.

**Camera**
- Vertical camera, lens 40–50mm.
- Ek **Empty** banayein, camera par "Track To" constraint → Empty ko move karein, camera follow karega.
- Har 3–4 sec par keyframe, interpolation **Ease In-Out**.
- Shake: camera par Noise modifier (sirf dhamake/toofan wale frames mein).

## 5. Kaam ki tarteeb (order)

1. ElevenLabs mein voice banayein → audio ki length note karein.
2. Blender mein audio import karein (Video Sequencer) takay animation voice ke saath sync ho.
3. Pehle **sirf block-out** (bina texture) poori animation banayein → viewport render se check karein.
4. Phir textures, lights, character lagayein.
5. Final render (raat bhar).
6. CapCut: video + voice + captions + SFX + halki music (YouTube Audio Library).
7. Export 1080x1920, upload.
