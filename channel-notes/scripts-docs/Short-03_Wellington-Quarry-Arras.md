**Title:** How a Hole Under a City Becomes a Secret Army Base (or: 24,000 Soldiers Vanished Underground)
**Length:** ~66 sec | 720x1280 test, 1080x1920 final | Voice: ElevenLabs (calm documentary)
**Pipeline:** Blender (cloud render)

> **Facts note** (verify on Wikipedia "Wellington Quarry" / "Battle of Arras (1917)" before upload):
> - Under Arras (France) were old medieval chalk quarries. In 1916–17 the **New Zealand Tunnelling Company** (with British tunnellers) linked them into a network of about 20 km.
> - The caves were named after New Zealand towns: Wellington, Auckland, Nelson, Blenheim, Christchurch…
> - It had electric light, running water, a light railway and a hospital (about 700 beds).
> - About **24,000 soldiers** were hidden there before the attack.
> - The attack came on **9 April 1917 at 5:30 am**, in snow and sleet; exits were opened close to the German front line.
> - **Monetization safety:** show soldiers advancing, explosions OK; no blood, no bodies, no flags or insignia.

## Voiceover script

> You dig a hole under a city that's being shelled.
> Down there, you find something nobody remembers. Medieval chalk quarries. Huge, empty caves.
> So you connect them. Cave to cave, with tunnels.
> But the chalk is soft, and the roof starts to crack.
> So you hold it up with timber, beam by beam.
> Above you, the enemy listens for digging.
> So you dig quietly, with hand tools, and they never hear a thing.
> You need light. So you run electric cables through the caves.
> You need water. So you pipe it in.
> You need to move fast. So you lay a railway.
> Soldiers will get hurt. So you build a hospital. Seven hundred beds. Underground.
> You name every cave after a town back home. Wellington. Auckland. Nelson.
> Then, one night, twenty-four thousand soldiers walk in. And disappear.
> Up above, the enemy sees an empty city.
> Below them, you dig the last few meters, right under their front line.
> At five thirty in the morning, in the snow, the exits blow open.
> And thousands of soldiers rise out of the ground, right on top of the enemy.
> Congratulations. You have built the tunnels under Arras.

(~175 words, ~64–68 sec)
